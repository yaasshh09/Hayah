# Brag Plan: Hayati (حياتي)

## What is this app?
Hayati is a family finance and planning web app for UAE households: one shared view of the family budget, the UAE occasion calendar, savings goals, bills, debts, UAE money tools, a kids' Save / Spend / Give mode, and a Gemini-powered assistant that knows the family's calendar.

## The angle
Family money in the UAE is scattered: rent here, school fees there, Eidiya in an envelope, Ramadan hosting on a group chat. Hayati pulls all of it into one calm place and ties every dirham to the moment it is meant for. The video is a hackathon demo for the ADU Software Engineering Innovation Cup 2K26 (theme: "Engineering Tomorrow's Families: Software Solutions for Connected and Caring UAE"), so it has to explain every feature in detail while still feeling like a launch film. Captions are written in my voice as the builder, first person, no narration.

## Hook (first 8 seconds)
Kinetic chaos. Real family expenses slam onto the screen one by one: "Rent." "School fees." "DEWA." "Eidiya." "Ramadan iftars." "Summer travel." The pile jitters and overflows. Line: "Every UAE family juggles all of this."

- The fragments (spreadsheet, calendar, bank SMS, group chat "did anyone pay DEWA?") get sucked into the center and the giant "Hayati" wordmark slams in on the first music drop (16.0s), with "حياتي" and "it means my life".
- The four Hayati family cards (Jiddo Khalid, Noura, Ahmed, Latifa) swing in on a clothesline.

- "A calendar that knows what Eid will cost." Occasion budget cards and the 26,400 AED set aside counter.
- Finance tab montage on the beat: Budget, Expenses, Debts, Bills, Occasions, Tools.
- UAE tools: loan calculator, end-of-service gratuity AED 60,000, Zakat AED 1,975.
- Adult / Child switch flipping on the drop at 95.85s, then Latifa's Save / Spend / Give jars filling.
- Hayati AI: a question typed letter by letter in the quiet section, the answer blooming on the 119.8s drop.
- Light to dark theme wipe, ADHD-friendly and high contrast toggles.

## Outro / punchline
Fast mosaic of every screen pulling back into a wall, then the end card: "Hayati حياتي. Make room for what matters." Bell on the 143.8s phrase.

## User flow worth showing
Enter Hayati as an adult → dashboard shows where the family stands → plan an occasion budget in the calendar → track and fix spending in Finance → switch to Child mode and see Latifa's jars → ask Hayati AI how to split the Eid buffer.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (2:44, 120 BPM), the only bundled track long enough
- Music treatment: 0.32 bed, dips under the AI typing section naturally, fades out 146-150s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (tempo 120.19). Phrases every 8 bars land at 0.02, 16.0, 31.96, 47.94, 63.9, 79.88, 95.85, 111.8, 119.8, 127.8, 143.8. Strong-cue locks: logo slam 16.0, Child switch 95.85, AI answer 119.8, end card 143.8. Scene cuts sit on bar lines (0.02 + n*1.997).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass drives background glow blobs and the logo halo. No visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: dense. Whoosh on every transition, punches on kinetic words, card sounds on screens, chips on count-ups, switch on toggles, keypresses on typing, bell on payoffs.
- Restraint rule: never stack more than three SFX inside 0.1s; keep bright sounds rare.
